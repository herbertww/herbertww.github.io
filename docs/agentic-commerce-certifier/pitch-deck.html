The Idea4 min
The solution, step by step

Scan the money leg. Certify it. Keep it fixed.

01 — Scan

A headless agent buys something

Discovery, an eight-way signature challenge, a real checkout session, delegated-token authorisation, mandate-scope negatives, idempotency, refund, dispute evidence. Merchant sandbox, test mode, compensating void on every auth.

02 — Certify

~120 checks, five pillars, one score

Deterministic, versioned, evidence-backed GreenLane Score 0–1000. Issued as a 90-day W3C Verifiable Credential, published to a public registry, revoked in minutes when a weekly re-run regresses.

03 — Remediate

Failures come back as fixes

A config change for Shopify; a pull request via GitHub App for custom stacks, carrying the check ID, evidence link, patch and a reproducing test. Plus a CI gate, so readiness can't silently rot.

The user journey — 40 seconds to a number, three weeks to a badge

DAY 1Paste a URL. Free report in 40 seconds with a provisional score and the three biggest gaps.
DAY 2Prove the domain by DNS TXT → unlock protocol tests → discover the WAF is blocking verified Mastercard and Visa agents.

Why a network buys this

Verifiable Intent assumes the merchant side holds. Nothing observes whether it does.

It changes fraud scoring, liability assignment and chargeback procedure — but only if the merchant integration actually enforces the mandate. A registered merchant accepting over-cap tokens is a liability carried blind.

The product for them

A per-inquiry readiness signal with stable reason codes

GL-P4-02-OVERCAP · GL-P2-10-BLOCKED · GL-P4-12-NOEVIDENCE — machine-actionable at authorisation, in the shape a network already sells VAS. It makes the liability framework enforceable rather than aspirational.

Feasibility2 min
Regulatory, technical and partner realities

We are a testing and assurance service. That is a licence decision, not an accident.

Regulatory

No MAS licence required, by design

MAS created no separate category for AI-initiated payments — existing consumer protection, AML and KYC apply, and the agent is an extension of the account holder. We hold no funds and move no real money, so no PS Act licence. PDPA residency in ap-southeast-1; SOC 2 Type II before the first FI contract.

Anchor

SAFR turns a white paper into a line item

Pillar P5 scores the merchant's audit envelope against SAFR's four pillars — policy-bound execution, real-time validation, auditability, interoperability. A merchant can be measured on it, not just briefed on it.

Legal

Assurance, not indemnity

The credential asserts observed behaviour of a named domain under a named rubric version at a timestamp — explicitly not a warranty. Backed by professional indemnity cover before the first paid certificate.

Kill risk 01

Agentic commerce stalls

Instant Checkout's retirement says the timing risk is real. Mitigation: L0/L1 has value the moment agents merely read stores — already true, already growing, independent of whether agent checkout wins.

Kill risk 02

A network or platform ships this free

The most likely outcome. Mitigation: be the neutral cross-network certifier. Mastercard won't certify Visa's protocol; Shopify won't certify a non-Shopify store. Neutrality is the one position no incumbent can occupy.

Kill risk 03

The credential never gets consumed

The one that actually matters. Mitigation: the merchant product is sold on merchant-facing ROI alone. Registry and risk-signal revenue are upside, not the base case.
